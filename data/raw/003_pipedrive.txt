Meeting intelligence built into every deal
Introducing Nova, Pipedrive’s latest feature. It captures what matters before, during and after every call and keeps your CRM up to date, right inside Pipedrive.
Briefed before:
Nova automatically pulls deal history and previous conversations into a pre-call brief. You always know exactly where things left off and what you need to do next.
Focused during:
It records and transcribes your calls so you don’t have to. You stay in the conversation, and Nova takes care of the notes.
Updated after:
It generates a recap and drafts CRM updates you can review and approve in one click – no manual entry, no catching up.
