Extract

Split

How it works
Built to read the way humans do.
Reducto’s multi-pass system utilizes both OCR and vision language models for unmatched accuracy and reliability.
See the whole document
r-1 understands layout, text, tables, figures, and formatting together in context.
Reason through complex structure
A unified parsing model handles dense tables, unusual layouts, annotations, and formatting-dependent information.
Produce structured, grounded output
r-1 returns accurate document structure, formatting, and granular citations ready for downstream workflows.
